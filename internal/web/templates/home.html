How it works

From “I saw it somewhere” to dinner.

01

Bring anything

A link, a TikTok or reel, a photo of grandma's card, a PDF, or your own voice. If a recipe lives in it, SaltyBytes pulls it out.

02

Get the recipe — just the recipe

Ingredients and steps, cleanly extracted. Units read your way: “2 cups (480 mL)”. Allergen flags for the whole family.

03

Make it yours

Fork it, tweak it, regenerate it. Your changes branch like a family tree, and the original is always one tap away.

The AI connector

Your AI assistant can cook with it.

SaltyBytes is a first-class connector for Claude and ChatGPT. Ask your assistant for dinner ideas and it searches real recipes, previews them, and saves keepers straight to your collection — no app-switching, no copy-paste.

{{.MCPURL}}

In Claude

  1. Settings → Connectors
  2. Add custom connector → paste the URL
  3. Sign in with your SaltyBytes account

In ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Connectors → Advanced
  2. Enable developer mode, add the URL
  3. Sign in — done
